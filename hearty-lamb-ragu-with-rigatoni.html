Hearty Lamb Ragu with Rigatoni

Ingredients
Traditional Pasta from Italy
  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil (plus 2 tbsp olive oil (1/4 + 2))
  • 2 whole carrots (finely chopped)
  • 1 whole onion (large, finely chopped)
  • 1 whole bell pepper (medium, finely chopped)
  • 4 oz pancetta (thickly sliced, cut into 1/4-inch dice)
  • 1 lb lamb shoulder (boneless, cut into 1/2-inch dice)
  • 3/4 cup red wine (dry)
  • 28 oz Italian tomatoes (1 can, coarsely chopped, juices reserved)
  • 1 cup chicken stock (or low-sodium broth)
  • 1 whole bay leaf
  • 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper
  • 1 dash kosher salt
  • 1 dash freshly ground pepper
  • 1 lb rigatoni
  • 1/2 cup percorino romano cheeze (freshly grated, for servicng)
Procedure
Follow these steps
  1. Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a medium Dutch oven over medium until shimmering. Add carrots, onion, and red bell pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and just beginning to brown, about 12 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the vegetables to a plate.
  2. Add the remaining 2 tablespoons oil to the Dutch oven. Add the pancetta and stir once or twice over moderately high heat until sizzling. Add the lamb and cook, stirring occasionally, until the liquid has evaporated and the meat is browned, 10 minutes. Return the vegetables to the pot. Add the red wine and simmer until evaporated, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Add the tomatoes and their juices, the chicken stock, bay leaf, and crushed red pepper. Season with salt and black pepper and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat to moderately low, cover partially, and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the lamb is very tender, 1 1/2 hours. Discard the bay leaf.
  3. In a large pot of boiling salted water, cook the rigatoni until al dente. Drain the rigatoni and toss with half of the lamb ragù. Serve the pasta in large bowls, passing the remaining lamb ragù and the pecorino cheese at the table.
Notes
Optional tips
  • Make ahead The lamb ragù can be refrigerated for up to five days or frozen for up to one month. Suggested pairing A soft, generous Montepulciano d'Abruzzo will combine well with the pungent lamb and sweet carrots.
